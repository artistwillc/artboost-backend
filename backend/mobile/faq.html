Frequently Asked Questions

What is ArtBoost AI?

ArtBoost AI is an AI-powered marketing workspace for artists and creators.

How do I connect stores or social platforms?

Use Connections inside ArtBoost. Supported services are authorized through their connection flow and then become available to campaigns and automations.

How do subscriptions work?

Start on Free, or select Starter, Pro or Business from Pricing. Paid checkout is processed securely through Stripe.

How do I get support?

Use AI Customer Support inside ArtBoost or email support@artboostai.com.
