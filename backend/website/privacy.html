ArtBoost AI Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

This Policy explains how ArtBoost AI handles information when you use its website, mobile application, integrations, scheduling tools, automations, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect account information such as your name, email address, user identifier, subscription status, and settings. Connected-platform data may include access tokens, refresh tokens, account identifiers, and connection status.

2. Content and Store Information

We may process artwork images, titles, descriptions, product links, store information, campaign content, schedules, and other materials you submit or import.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate ArtBoost AI, authenticate accounts, import products, generate content, publish or schedule posts at your direction, provide support, maintain security, and manage subscriptions.

4. Payments

Payment processing is handled securely through Stripe. ArtBoost AI does not sell your personal information.

5. Third-Party Platforms

When you connect a third-party platform, information may be exchanged with that platform as necessary to authenticate or perform actions you request.

6. Data Retention and Security

Information is retained as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain records, support integrations, and meet legal or security requirements.

7. Disconnecting Accounts

You can disconnect supported social platforms and stores through ArtBoost AI.

8. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of ArtBoost AI account data by emailing support@artboostai.com. Requests are generally processed within 30 days.

9. Children

ArtBoost AI is not intended for children under 13.

10. Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to support@artboostai.com.
