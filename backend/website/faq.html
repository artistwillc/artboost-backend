ArtBoost AI FAQ

Common questions about using ArtBoost AI.

What is ArtBoost AI?

ArtBoost AI helps artists and online sellers connect stores, organize artwork, generate marketing content, publish campaigns, schedule posts, and manage automations.

How do I connect a social platform?

Open Connect, choose a supported platform, complete authorization, return to ArtBoost, and refresh the connection status.

How do I connect a store?

Open Connect and choose the appropriate store integration. Supported stores may use direct authorization, a store URL, or catalog import.

Can ArtBoost AI publish automatically?

Supported connected platforms can be used in Campaign Manager and Store Automations, subject to your plan and the permissions granted by each platform.

Why do I sometimes need to reconnect?

Third-party access tokens can expire or be revoked. Reconnect the platform from Connect when ArtBoost reports an invalid or expired connection.

Does ArtBoost AI own my artwork?

No. You retain ownership of the artwork and content you provide.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Use the subscription or billing controls available in ArtBoost AI.

How do I get support?

Use Customer Service inside ArtBoost AI or email support@artboostai.com.
