Frequently Asked Questions

What is ArtBoost AI?

ArtBoost AI is an AI-powered marketing workspace for artists and creators. It brings content generation, connected stores, social publishing, scheduling, automation, analytics, creator tools and support into one workflow.

How do I connect stores or social platforms?

Use the Connections area inside ArtBoost. Supported connections are authorized through the platform's connection flow, then become available to campaigns and automations.

How do subscriptions work?

Start on Free, or select Starter, Pro or Business from the Pricing controls. Paid checkout is processed securely through Stripe.

How do I get support?

Use AI Customer Support inside ArtBoost or email support@artboostai.com.
