ArtBoost AI FAQ

Common questions about using ArtBoost AI.

What is ArtBoost AI?

ArtBoost AI helps artists and online sellers connect supported stores and social platforms, organize artwork and product information, generate marketing content, publish campaigns, schedule posts, and manage automations.

How do I connect a social platform?

Open Connect, choose a supported platform, complete that platform’s authorization flow, return to ArtBoost, and confirm the connection status.

What information does ArtBoost receive from TikTok?

Only information permitted by the TikTok scopes you authorize. The current TikTok integration may use basic profile information to identify your connected account and posting permissions to upload or publish eligible content when you request it or configure an authorized automation.

Can ArtBoost publish automatically?

Supported platforms can be used in campaigns, schedules, and automations, subject to your plan, the permissions you grant, your configuration, and the rules or availability of each connected platform.

How do I disconnect TikTok or another platform?

Use ArtBoost’s connection controls where available and, if needed, revoke ArtBoost’s access directly from the connected platform’s account or security settings.

Does ArtBoost AI own my artwork?

No. You retain ownership of the artwork and content you provide. You authorize ArtBoost to process that content as needed to provide the features you request.

How do I request deletion of my data?

Email support@artboostai.com from the email associated with your account. See the Privacy Policy for more information.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Use the subscription or billing controls available in ArtBoost AI. Cancellation generally prevents future renewals; see the Terms of Service for details.

How do I get support?

Use Customer Service inside ArtBoost AI or email support@artboostai.com.
