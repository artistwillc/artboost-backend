Delete Your ArtBoost AI Account

ArtBoost AI users may request permanent deletion of their ArtBoost AI account and associated account data. This page explains how to submit a deletion request and what happens after the request is verified.

Important: Canceling an ArtBoost AI subscription stops future subscription billing but does not, by itself, delete your ArtBoost AI account. Account deletion is a separate request.

How to request account deletion

  1. Email support@artboostai.com with the subject ArtBoost AI Account Deletion Request.
  2. Send the request from the email address associated with your ArtBoost AI account whenever possible.
  3. State clearly that you want your ArtBoost AI account and associated account data deleted.
  4. ArtBoost AI may contact you to verify account ownership before processing the request.
Request Account Deletion

Data deleted with your account

After a verified deletion request is processed, ArtBoost AI will delete or de-identify account data under its control that is no longer required for a legitimate retention purpose. This may include your ArtBoost account profile and settings, stored content and artwork associated with the account, imported product or listing information, campaign and scheduling information, and stored connection information for supported third-party integrations.

Data that may be retained

Some information may be retained where reasonably necessary for legal, tax, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, billing, payment-dispute, audit, or dispute-resolution obligations. Retention periods vary according to the type of record and the applicable requirement. Backup or security copies may also remain for a limited period until they are deleted or overwritten through normal retention processes.

Third-party services connected to ArtBoost AI may retain information independently under their own policies. Deleting your ArtBoost AI account does not automatically delete information held by services such as social networks, ecommerce platforms, app stores, or payment processors.

Processing time

ArtBoost AI generally aims to complete verified account-deletion requests within 30 days, subject to information that must be retained for the purposes described above.

Need help?

For questions about account or data deletion, contact support@artboostai.com. See the ArtBoost AI Privacy Policy for additional information about privacy and data handling.
